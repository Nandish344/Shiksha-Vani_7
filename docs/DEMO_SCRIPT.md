# 5-minute demo script

Open two windows: **Teacher** on a laptop (Chrome), **Student (Sona)** on a phone or a narrow browser window.

1. **The problem (20 s).** Landing page. "Sona speaks Santali; her lessons are in Hindi." Point at the word relay:
   one Hindi word arriving in four mother tongues.
2. **Live class (90 s).** Teacher: Live class > Go live. Student: home shows the red banner, tap *Join class*.
   Teacher taps the mic (or a phrase chip). Student sees the caption instantly. Student taps *I did not get this*;
   it appears on the teacher's screen. Teacher clicks the pencil on the Santali tile, types a translation, Save:
   **it appears on the child's phone at once** and is remembered for next time.
3. **Honesty and trust (30 s).** Show the "draft" tags. "Machine output is never shown as verified. A teacher who
   speaks the language approves it in Content review."
4. **Lesson, quiz, offline (60 s).** Student: Lessons > save one for offline. Turn on airplane mode (or DevTools >
   Offline), finish the quiz. Pill shows "1 waiting to sync". Reconnect: it syncs and the teacher dashboard updates.
5. **Reading practice + tutor (45 s).** Speak: read a Hindi sentence, words turn green/red. Ask: "what do roots do?"
   and "water in Santali".
6. **Teacher insight (60 s).** Overview: 32 children, early warnings. Open **Sona Murmu (58%)** and **Birsa Ho
   (61%)**, the same students as slide 6. Read the suggested action. Open the bilingual report > Print.
7. **Parent (30 s).** Parent login: report in Santali/Hindi, *Listen to report*, quick message to the teacher.

## Likely judge questions
* *Do you support Mundari/Ho/Kurukh translation?* Through Bhashini where a model exists, plus teacher-authored
  lines that become a reusable translation memory. Anything unverified is labelled.
* *Accuracy?* Nothing is shown as "checked" without a native-speaker teacher; the review queue is the safeguard.
* *No internet?* Lessons, quizzes and practice run offline and sync later (demo it).
* *Cost / devices?* Runs in a browser on low-end Android; no app-store install.
